Indigo & Zari

The CreoTask design system. Indigo-ink grounds, zari-gold accents taken from the logo, Anek Latin type, and a stitched thread for anything that moves through stages. This page is for review and only exists in development.

Colour

Components use semantic roles (background, surface, text, accent), never raw hex. Gold is reserved for primary actions, progress and earned status. It isn't decoration.

Type

One family, Anek Latin, used at two widths. Display and headings run wide and heavy; reading text stays at normal width. Cinzel appears only in the wordmark.
