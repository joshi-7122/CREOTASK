Two ways in.
One honest process.

The same project, step by step, from each side.

If you're hiring

  1. Write a structured briefScope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and a budget split into milestones that add up exactly.
  2. Compare on evidenceVerified skills, relevant work samples and delivery history, side by side.
  3. Agree exact termsBoth of you see the same scope, milestones, fees and dates before work starts.
  4. Review each milestoneAccept the work, or ask for a revision within what you agreed.

If you're freelancing

  1. Build a profile from evidenceSkills, work samples and availability. Submit evidence to get skills verified.
  2. Apply with the work that fitsA message written for the brief, plus the samples that match it.
  3. Deliver in one workspaceMessages, files, versions and revisions stay with the project.
  4. Grow your recordAccepted, paid work moves you towards the next tier.

CreoStart,
for your first projects.

From naming your first skill to delivering your first project, with a clear line between paid work and practice.

  1. List what you can do: skills, tools and how long you've used them. Skills you declare yourself are labelled as self-declared.

  2. Add work samples or practice pieces. Paid, personal or practice work all count, and clients compare real samples, not just ratings.

  3. Submit evidence and a reviewer checks it. Verified means a person looked at your work, not that you paid.

  4. Apply to a paid CreoStart entry project from a real client, or try a practice challenge from CreoTask, which is always marked unpaid.

  5. Work milestone by milestone in one shared workspace. The client accepts the work or asks for a revision within what you agreed.

  6. Accepted, paid work and verified skills move you towards the next tier. Tiers are earned from delivered work and can't be bought.

Questions, answered plainly.

Can I use CreoTask today?

You can create an account, verify your email and build your profile now. Paid projects open once our payment partner is approved. Until then, no money moves through CreoTask.

What does it cost?

Creating an account and building a profile is free. Before you accept or fund any project, you'll see the exact fee, any tax and what the freelancer receives. Nothing is charged by surprise.

How do payments work?

Every project has a fixed price split into milestones. Each milestone is paid through an approved payment provider, and you can always see whether an amount is pending, funded, released or refunded.

How are tiers decided?

From delivered work: completed paid projects, verified skills, ratings and on-time delivery. Tiers can't be bought, they never override what a project requires, and every tier decision comes with a reason you can appeal.

What if I disagree with a decision?

People make the big calls. Tier changes, skill verification and disputes each come with a stated reason, and you can appeal them.

I'm new. Will anyone hire me?

We can't promise work, but qualified newcomers get a fair look alongside established names, and nobody is shown for a project they can't do. CreoStart has paid entry projects meant for first-timers. Practice challenges are always clearly marked as unpaid.

Who can join?

Anyone in India who is at least 18. One account can both hire and work; you choose which role you're using at any time.
